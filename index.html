Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Hopeo PR ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Hopeo PR mobile application (the "App"), a happy hour and craft beer guide for Puerto Rico. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, who processes it on our behalf, and the choices you have.

Hopeo is a connected app. To sign you in, sync your check-ins across devices, verify that you are a Puerto Rico local, manage your subscription, and keep the catalog current, we collect information and store it on servers operated by our service providers. This section replaces an earlier version of this policy that described Hopeo as storing everything only on your device. That description no longer reflects how the App works.

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

Hopeo uses Sign in with Apple. Apple gives us a unique identifier for your account and, only if you choose to share it, your name — which becomes your initial display name. We do not request or store your email address. The identifier is stored in Firebase Authentication and in a user record in our database so we can recognize you when you sign back in.

2.2 Profile and Preferences

We store the profile you create in the App: your username, display name, optional bio, your access tier (Local, Visitor, or Guest), and the taste preferences you select during setup. Your username is public to other users wherever your activity appears — for example on Community Routes and reviews.

2.3 Phone Number (only if you verify as a local)

Free local access requires confirming a Puerto Rico phone number. When you request a code, we send your phone number to Twilio, which generates and delivers the SMS and checks the code you enter. We keep a short-lived record linking an opaque challenge identifier to that number so the App never has to transmit the raw number a second time; that record expires shortly after verification. We use the number only to confirm a Puerto Rico area code. We do not use it for marketing and we do not send you SMS messages other than the verification code.

2.4 Location Data

The App asks for location permission to:

  • Show nearby venues and happy hour deals, and center the map where you are
  • Confirm you are physically at a venue before allowing a check-in
  • Send you alerts about happy hours starting near you (limited to roughly a 10-mile radius)
  • Optionally, with separate "Always" permission that we request only after you have used the App, notify you when you arrive at a venue so you can check in

When you complete a check-in, the latitude and longitude of that check-in are saved to our servers as part of the check-in record. We do not continuously track or log your movements, and we do not build advertising profiles from your location. You can revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings; the map and nearby features will stop working, but the rest of the App continues to function.

2.5 Your Activity and Content

The following are stored on our servers so they persist across devices and, where noted, are visible to other users:

  • Check-ins (venue, timestamp, coordinates, and any optional notes or rating)
  • Badges, passport progress, and league standings
  • Reviews you write about venues (public)
  • Custom routes you build, and Community Routes you publish (published routes are public)
  • Plans you create and RSVPs to plans you are invited to
  • Votes and reports you submit about a venue or about another user's content, including any note you write. If you attach a photo to a report, that photo is uploaded to our servers so our moderation team can review the evidence — which is what lets us act on reports of objectionable content within the timeframe described in the Terms. Report photos are never shown to other users; only our team can see them. A copy also stays on your device.
  • Venue claims, if you are an owner claiming your business

2.6 Purchases

Subscriptions are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes your payment; we never see or receive your card details. We use RevenueCat to track whether your subscription is active, which involves sharing an app-specific user identifier and your purchase status with them.

2.7 Usage Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used so we can improve it. This records events such as opening a venue, tapping or saving a deal, completing a check-in, starting a route, viewing the paywall, and completing a purchase, along with your access tier. Firebase Analytics also collects standard device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, app version, an Apple-provided vendor identifier, and coarse region). We do not use this data for advertising and we do not sell it.

2.8 Abuse Prevention

We use Firebase App Check to confirm that requests to our servers come from a genuine copy of the App rather than an automated script.

2.9 Customer Support

If you email us for support, we receive whatever you send us (such as your email address and a description of the issue) and use it solely to respond.
