Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Hopeo PR ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Hopeo PR mobile application (the "App"), a craft beer hopping guide for Puerto Rico. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights with respect to that information.

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Display name and username
  • Email address
  • Subscription tier (Local, Visitor, or Guest)
  • Profile bio (optional)

2.2 Location Data

The App uses your device's location to:

  • Show nearby venues and happy hour deals on the map
  • Verify your physical proximity to a venue before allowing a check-in (within 150 meters)
  • Enable route navigation features

Location is accessed only while the App is in use ("When In Use" permission). We do not track your location in the background.

2.3 User Content

When you perform a check-in, you may optionally provide:

  • Beer name and rating
  • Personal notes about the visit
  • Photos or videos of the venue or beverage

This content is associated with your account and may be used to display your activity history within the App.

2.4 Usage Data

We may automatically collect limited technical data, including:

  • App version and device operating system
  • Crash reports and error logs (for debugging purposes only)
  • General feature usage patterns (e.g., which tabs are used most)

This data is collected in aggregate and is not linked to individual identities.

2.5 Customer Support

If you contact us for support, we collect the information you provide (such as your email and description of the issue) solely to respond to your request.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App's core features
  • Verify proximity for check-ins and route navigation
  • Display your check-in history, badges, and achievements
  • Personalize your experience (e.g., saved deals, preferred settings)
  • Send essential service communications (account confirmations, security alerts)
  • Respond to support requests
  • Monitor and fix crashes and performance issues

We do not use your information for third-party advertising or sell your personal data to any third party.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted vendors who help us operate the App (e.g., cloud hosting, crash analytics) under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Hopeo PR, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user information may be transferred as part of that transaction, with prior notice provided to users.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Check-in history, ratings, and notes are stored until you delete them or close your account.

To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the email address listed in Section 10. We will process your request within 30 days.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS) and access controls on our servers.

No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your Hopeo PR account.

7. Children's Privacy

Hopeo PR is intended for users who are 21 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 21. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information ("right to be forgotten").
  • Portability: Request a portable copy of your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Opt-out: Withdraw consent for optional data processing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in Section 10. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Third-Party Services

The App uses Apple's MapKit framework to display maps and location data. Apple's use of data through MapKit is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy (apple.com/privacy).

The App does not currently integrate third-party advertising SDKs, analytics platforms, or social login providers beyond what is described in this policy.
