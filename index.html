Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

MSBI Group LLC (doing business as Hopeo) ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Hopeo mobile application (the "App"), a happy hour and craft beer guide for Puerto Rico. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, who processes it on our behalf, and the choices you have.

Hopeo is a connected app. To sign you in, verify that you are a Puerto Rico local, manage your subscription, publish what you choose to share with other users, and keep the catalog current, we collect information and store it on servers operated by our service providers. This replaces an earlier version of this policy that described Hopeo as storing everything only on your device.

Some things still never leave your iPhone — your check-ins, your badges and passport progress, and the routes you build for yourself. Section 2.5 lists exactly what stays local and what does not, and Section 2.4 explains what happens to your location.

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

Hopeo uses Sign in with Apple. Apple gives us a unique identifier for your account and, only if you choose to share it, your name — which becomes your initial display name. We do not request or store your email address. The identifier is stored on your device and is attached to the content you publish (see 2.5), so your posts stay yours. We do not keep a separate user profile on our servers.

On Android, the App creates an anonymous account identifier through Firebase Authentication when you first open it; it carries no name, email or phone number. If we later add Google or Apple sign-in on Android, we will ask you first and update this policy to say exactly what your provider shares with us.

2.2 Profile and Preferences

We store the profile you create in the App: your username, display name, optional bio, your access tier (Local, Visitor, or Guest), and the taste preferences you select during setup. Your username is public to other users wherever your activity appears — for example on Community Routes and reviews.

2.3 Phone Number (only if you verify as a local)

Free local access requires confirming a Puerto Rico phone number. When you request a code, we send your phone number to Twilio, which generates and delivers the SMS and checks the code you enter. We keep a record linking an opaque challenge identifier to that number only while the code is pending, so the App never has to transmit the raw number a second time; that record is deleted as soon as you verify, and in any case within 24 hours. To limit how often codes can be requested we keep a salted one-way hash of the number for up to one hour; it cannot be reversed to recover your number. We use the number only to confirm a Puerto Rico area code. We do not use it for marketing and we do not send you SMS messages other than the verification code.

2.4 Location Data

On Android the App only uses location in the foreground, and only when you tap "Hacer check-in": it reads your position once to confirm you are within 150 m of the venue, compares it on your device, and keeps a rounded (~110 m) value inside the local check-in record. It never requests background location.

The App asks for location permission to:

  • Show nearby venues and happy hour deals, and center the map where you are
  • Confirm you are physically at a venue before allowing a check-in
  • Send you alerts about happy hours starting near you (limited to roughly a 10-mile radius)
  • Optionally, with separate "Always" permission that we request only after you have used the App, notify you when you arrive at a venue so you can check in

Your location never leaves your device. When you complete a check-in, your device uses your coordinates to confirm you are actually at the venue, and the check-in record — coordinates included — is stored only in the App on your iPhone. We do not receive it, we do not continuously track or log your movements, and we do not build advertising profiles from your location. You can revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings; the map and nearby features will stop working, but the rest of the App continues to function.

2.5 Your Activity and Content

These never leave your iPhone. We do not receive them, they are not backed up to us, and they are gone if you delete the App:

  • Check-in records — the coordinates, the exact time, and any note, rating or photo you added. (A check-in does send one thing to our servers: a monthly count of your check-ins at that venue, under your display name, for the public leaderboards described below. It never includes coordinates, times, notes or photos.)
  • Badges and passport progress
  • Routes you build for yourself, and happy hours you save

These are stored on our servers so they persist and, where noted, are visible to other users:

  • Reviews you write about venues (public)
  • Community Routes you publish (public)
  • Weekly league standings and Local Legend counts — a per-venue, per-month tally of your check-ins with your chosen display name next to it (public: anyone using Hopeo can see which display names have checked in at a venue and how often; pick a display name that does not identify you if you prefer)
  • Who follows you and whom you follow — when you follow someone, your display name, your chosen accent color and whether you are a subscriber are stored next to their profile, so their followers list can show you (public). Unfollowing removes it; deleting your account replaces your name with "Hopero".
  • Drink boards you share for a venue (public)
  • Plans you create and RSVPs to plans you are invited to
  • Votes and reports you submit about a venue or about another user's content, including any note you write. If you attach a photo to a report, that photo is uploaded to our servers so our moderation team can review the evidence — which is what lets us act on reports of objectionable content within the timeframe described in the Terms. Report photos are never shown to other users; only our team can see them. A copy also stays on your device.
  • Venue claims, if you are an owner claiming your business — your name, role, contact email, optional phone and note. If you prove ownership with a photo of a business permit you send it to us by email; we delete that image once the claim is verified and in any case within 90 days, and it is never stored in the App. The claim record itself is retained as a record of the verification we performed.

2.6 Purchases

On Android, subscriptions will be sold through Google Play Billing; Google processes the payment and we never see your card details. On iOS, subscriptions are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes your payment; we never see or receive your card details. We use RevenueCat to track whether your subscription is active, which involves sharing an app-specific user identifier and your purchase status with them.

2.7 Usage Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics and PostHog to understand how the App is used so we can improve it. PostHog receives a randomly generated identifier that is not linked to your name, your Apple account, or your email address. This records events such as opening a venue, tapping or saving a deal, completing a check-in, starting a route, viewing the paywall, and completing a purchase, along with your access tier. Firebase Analytics also collects standard device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, app version, an Apple-provided vendor identifier, and coarse region). We also use Firebase Crashlytics to receive automatic reports when the App crashes; these contain device and app state, not the content you create, and expire after 90 days. We do not use this data for advertising and we do not sell it.

2.8 Notifications

Most of Hopeo's alerts — a happy hour about to start, arriving at a venue, a drink on your list — are scheduled by your own device and never reach us. One is different: if someone follows you, we send that alert from our servers. To do that, Apple gives your device a push token — an address that identifies this installation, not you — and we store it in our database, together with your account identifier, so we know where to deliver. The token changes on its own when you reinstall or restore your device, it is never shared with anyone outside the providers listed in Section 4, and it is deleted when you delete your account. The alert we send never contains text written by another user. If you turn notifications off in iOS Settings, no token is created and nothing is sent.

2.9 Abuse Prevention

We use Firebase App Check to confirm that requests to our servers come from a genuine copy of the App rather than an automated script.

2.10 Customer Support

If you email us for support, we receive whatever you send us (such as your email address and a description of the issue) and use it solely to respond.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. Phone verification records are deleted when you verify, and in any case within 24 hours.

You can delete your account from within the App (Profile → Settings). Deleting your account erases your profile, check-ins, saved happy hours, badges, and routes from your device, and resets the identifiers used by our analytics and subscription providers.

Anything you posted publicly — a community route, a venue review, a hangout plan, a Local Legend or weekly-league entry — is unlinked from you rather than erased: your name is replaced with "Hopero" and the identifiers that tie the post to you are deleted. The text of a review stays, because other people and the venue rely on it, but nothing in it points back to you. Aggregate figures (for example, a venue's total check-in count) also remain, in a form that no longer identifies you.

Deleting the App from your device alone does not delete your account — use the in-App deletion, or request it without the App at legal.hopeo.app/eliminar-cuenta (or by emailing prhopeo@gmail.com). We complete deletion within 30 days. Purchase and subscription records are retained by Google Play, Apple and our subscription processor for tax and fraud-prevention purposes; SMS delivery logs are retained by our verification provider under its own retention policy; anonymised crash reports expire after 90 days. None of these can be used to rebuild your account or identify you inside the App.

What deletion does not remove. Your username handle stays reserved so nobody can take it to impersonate you; it no longer points to any profile. Drink boards you shared for a venue remain, without your name. Business-ownership claims you submitted are retained as a record of the verification we performed. Analytics events already recorded may persist in aggregate, no longer tied to an identifier.

Reports are an exception. Reports you file — including any photo you attached as evidence — are moderation records, and we retain them after account deletion so we can enforce our Terms and keep a record of the action we took. They are never shown to other users.
