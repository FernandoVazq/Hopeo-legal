Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Hopeo PR ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Hopeo PR mobile application (the "App"), a craft beer hopping guide for Puerto Rico. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information on your device and your rights with respect to that information.

The App stores your information locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on our servers.

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

All information described below is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us or to any third party.

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, the App stores on your device:

  • Display name and username
  • Subscription tier (Local, Visitor, or Guest)
  • Profile bio (optional)

If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple provides a unique identifier that the App stores on your device to keep you signed in. You may choose to hide your email through Apple's private relay; we do not require or store your email address.

2.2 Location Data

The App uses your device's location to:

  • Show nearby venues and happy hour deals on the map
  • Verify your physical proximity to a venue before allowing a check-in (within 150 meters)
  • Enable route navigation features

Location is processed on your device only while the App is in use ("When In Use" permission). We do not track your location in the background, and your location is never transmitted to us.

2.3 User Content

When you perform a check-in, you may optionally provide:

  • Beer name and rating
  • Personal notes about the visit
  • Photos or videos of the venue or beverage

This content is stored locally on your device to display your activity history within the App. It is not uploaded to us.

2.4 Customer Support

If you choose to contact us for support by email, we receive the information you provide (such as your email address and description of the issue) solely to respond to your request. This is the only situation in which information leaves your device, and only because you initiate the email yourself.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Information stored on your device is used to:

  • Provide and operate the App's core features
  • Verify proximity for check-ins and route navigation
  • Display your check-in history, badges, and achievements
  • Personalize your experience (e.g., saved deals, preferred settings)

We do not use your information for advertising, analytics, or marketing, and we do not sell or share your personal data with any third party.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Because your data stays on your device, we have nothing to sell, rent, or trade. We do not share your personal information with third parties. The only information we ever receive is what you voluntarily send us by email when requesting support.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device for as long as you keep the App installed. You are in full control: deleting individual check-ins, routes, or your account from within the App removes that data from your device. Deleting the App from your device removes all associated data.

6. Data Security

Because your information is stored locally on your device, it is protected by your device's own security (such as your passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID). We do not maintain servers that store your personal data, which significantly reduces the risk of a remote data breach.

7. Children's Privacy

Hopeo PR is intended for users who are 21 years of age or older, consistent with Puerto Rico's legal drinking age. The App is not directed to anyone under the age of 21.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Because your data is stored locally on your device, you have direct and complete control over it at all times:

  • Access: All of your data is visible to you directly within the App.
  • Correction: You can edit or update your information at any time within the App.
  • Deletion: You can delete individual entries, your account, or the entire App, which removes the associated data from your device.

If you have questions about your rights, you may contact us using the information in Section 10.

9. Third-Party Services

The App uses Apple's MapKit framework to display maps and location data. Apple's use of data through MapKit is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy (apple.com/privacy).

The App does not currently integrate third-party advertising SDKs, analytics platforms, or social login providers beyond what is described in this policy.
